Provenance Wines · Design system

Wines that know where they come from.

Personal in character. Precise in everything else.

The Provenance Wines Visual Identity (v1.0, 29 October 2026) applied to the website. Like every Belmonte Beverage Group brand, it is a theme of the shared frontend: the same components and layout, styled for Provenance.

01 · Introduction

Where this comes from

The brand book “A sense of place” (Provenance Wines Visual Identity, proposal v1.0) owns the identity: signature, colours, typography, photography and tone. This page applies it to the website components and checks it on screen.

Source

Visual Identity v1.0

Keep the signature, give it a stronger frame, make provenance visible. Warm Ivory and Forest Green are the everyday foundation; Signature Pink identifies.

Rule

Style, not structure

Provenance reuses the BBG components and CMS content. Only colours, type, shapes and small interaction details change.

Status

Prototype

02 · Voice & audience

Knowledgeable. Human. Direct.

Write like a well-informed partner across the table. Use concrete details, active sentences and measured confidence. A partner to producers, a clear guide for the trade.

Who we speak to
  • Norwegian restaurants, wine bars and hotels
  • Producers looking for a partner in Norway
  • Not consumers: Norwegian law bans alcohol advertising, so the site speaks to the trade
How we write
  • Write this: “We work closely with the producers we represent.”
  • Avoid this: “The world’s finest wines, curated for unforgettable moments.”
  • Use the slogan exactly: Wines that know where they come from.
03 · Visual language

Logo

The signature stays: an existing asset with a human hand. Preserve the full mark, its rhythm and its original proportions. Signature Pink on a solid Forest field is the master identity.

Pink on Forest · preferred
Forest on light · preferred
Earth Red · warm alternative

Clear space and size

Do
  • Pink on a solid Forest field; Forest on light backgrounds
  • White reversed out of Earth Red as a utility version
  • Endorse with “Part of Belmonte Beverage Group” in Manrope, kept separate from the signature
Don’t
  • Stretch, rotate, crop strokes or add effects
  • Place the logo on busy photography; use a solid panel beside the image
  • Use Pink on Ivory (1.84:1) except at large scale after a legibility check

The prototype recolours the supplied PNG artwork to the brand book’s HEX values. The brand book says the geometry comes from the supplied SVG; that SVG is still needed for the app.

04 · Visual language

Colour

Three colours do most of the work: Warm Ivory as the breathing space, Forest Green as the structure and Signature Pink as the personal accent. Keep the supporting cast small. Suggested balance: 55% Ivory, 25% Forest, 10% Pink, 5% Stone, 3% Sage, 2% Earth.

Core

Supporting

Approved pairings

Contrast ratios are calculated live from the tokens. Body text needs at least 4.5:1 (WCAG AA); large headings at least 3:1.

05 · Visual language

Typography

Modern clarity, an editorial accent. Manrope carries headlines, body copy, navigation, facts and tables. Newsreader is a restrained second voice for the slogan, a short introduction or an occasional quotation. Sentence case; paragraphs left aligned at 45–75 characters.

06 · Visual language

Shape & layout

A calm, contemporary editorial system: square images, thin rules and generous space. The Origin Index — a small label, a rule and open corners — organises factual detail. Buttons are Forest with Ivory text; links are Earth Red with an underline. The page layout is unchanged from the agreed prototype.

07 · Visual language

Icons

Thin Forest linework with rounded ends. The Provenance arrow is long and horizontal, like the underline stroke of the signature. No ornamental crests, grape icons or invented maps.

08 · Visual language

Photography

Be close enough to notice. Real people and real places: natural gestures, hands and tools, vineyards, cellars and everyday spaces. Edit lightly — truthful greens, natural skin tones, no heavy filters. The photos in this prototype are placeholders chosen by the client (Les Quatre Piliers and others) to show the direction; they are not the final website photography.

11 · Open questions

What we need to confirm

Alignment

The brand book prefers left-aligned paragraphs and shared edges; the agreed layout centres the intros, product page and footer. Keep the layout, or left-align?

Footer colour

The footer now uses the brand’s Forest Green #1B2B1D instead of the interim Belmonte green #162A19. They are almost the same; confirm.

SVG logo

The master SVG is needed for the app’s header, footer and favicon (the PW utility tile is optional).

Photography rights

The photos are placeholders taken from lesquatrepiliers.com and the client’s folder. Rights and producer approval are needed before any public use.

Copy

Only the core introduction and slogan come from the brand book; the rest is placeholder text in its voice.

Content

Real producers, wines, articles and people in Crystallize.
